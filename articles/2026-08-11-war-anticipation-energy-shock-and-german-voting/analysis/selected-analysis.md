# Selected Analysis Plan

## Claim boundary

Describe municipality-level political changes across an early-invasion election, a first post-pipeline-stop election, and a later election. Evaluate the later Brandenburg PCK-proximity gradient with SPUR, while treating distance as an exploratory geographic descriptor rather than an energy-price treatment.

## Spatial panels

1. **Early invasion:** Schleswig-Holstein Landtag, 2017 to 8 May 2022. Match common official polling-district identifiers, use the 2022 district-to-municipality key, aggregate to municipality, and disclose unmatched electorate coverage. Common identifiers are not assumed to prove unchanged boundaries.
2. **Immediately post-break:** Schleswig-Holstein local elections, 2018 to 14 May 2023. Match municipality results. Turnout is the broadly comparable outcome; party changes require positive votes in both elections and explicit candidature coverage.
3. **Later adjustment:** Brandenburg Landtag, 2019 to 22 September 2024. Match official municipality aggregates, join BKG geometry, and calculate straight-line distance from polygon representative points to the UBA PRTR PCK coordinate. Report unweighted and valid-vote-weighted quartile summaries and centroid sensitivity.

Lower Saxony constituency and Berlin aggregate comparisons remain context. WSI aggregates provide the national sentiment timeline.

## SPUR specification and checks

- Exploratory formula: `AfD change (2019-2024) ~ PCK proximity`, with proximity equal to negative representative-point distance in 100 km.
- Run `spur-python` with q=10, 20,000 simulations, seed 42, and the Becker-Boll-Voth 10% rule. Select levels only if I(0) is not rejected and I(1) is rejected; otherwise select the branch jointly transforming outcome and regressor. Use SCPC intervals.
- Report both branches. Interpret them only as spatial associations. SPUR addresses persistent spatial variation and inference, not confounding, exposure validity, or mediation.
- Check IDs, positive denominators, count inequalities, share bounds, matched units, electorate/candidature coverage, and spatial joins in code.
