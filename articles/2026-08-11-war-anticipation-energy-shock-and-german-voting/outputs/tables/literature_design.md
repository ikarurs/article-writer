# Literature and design comparison

| Study / component | Exposure | Political outcome | Identification / comparison | Contribution | Main limit |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Konc et al. (2026) | Realised household electricity-instalment increase | Individual party support and attitudes | Staggered difference-in-differences using later-treated households | Causal benchmark; reported +7.5 pp AfD support for above-median increases | External validity to places, fuels, and elections is not automatic |
| Gregor and Haucap (2025) | 2022 fuel-tax cut and wholesale fuel prices | None | Pass-through analysis | Shows regional price pass-through must be measured | Tax cut and refinery margins, not voting |
| Kirchner (2026) | Predetermined Berlin heating technology | Polling-district 2021/2023 election change | Cross-district exposure gradient | Closest spatial-voting overlap and main novelty constraint | Preliminary paper; unusual repeat-election setting |
| This municipality package | Calendar phase and PCK distance | Same-office municipality changes in SH and BB | Stable-key matching, maps, descriptive distance quartiles | Separates early invasion, first post-break, and later local outcomes below Bundesland level | No observed local price first stage; distance is not treatment |
| Full article upgrade | Realised station/municipality price shock | Same-type municipality election changes and GLES timing | Price first stage, phase interactions, fixed effects, pre-period placebos, spatially robust inference | Tests whether the household mechanism generalises geographically | Requires authenticated historical fuel-price data and boundary harmonisation |
