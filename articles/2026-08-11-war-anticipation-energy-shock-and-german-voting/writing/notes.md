# Writing notes

- Lead with the municipality chronology, not Bundesland totals: Schleswig-Holstein in May 2022, Schleswig-Holstein local elections in May 2023, and Brandenburg in September 2024.
- Use the WSI 12% → 16% → 23% series only to establish national timing. It is published aggregate evidence, not local switching.
- State the early panel's exact construction: 2,485 stable precincts, 94.00% of the 2017 electorate, aggregated into 1,097 municipality units.
- Treat the 2023 local-election party results cautiously. AfD is comparable in only five municipalities because local candidature changes; turnout is the defensible map.
- Describe the Brandenburg distance pattern as non-monotonic or U-shaped. Do not cherry-pick the closest quartile while ignoring the high farthest quartile.
- Explain SPUR briefly: both outcome tests fail to reject at 10%, so the decision rule selects joint transformation; the transformed SCPC interval includes zero.
- Make explicit that SPUR handles spatial persistence and inference, not confounding or whether refinery distance is a valid price exposure.
- Use Konc et al. as the causal household benchmark, never as confirmation of the municipality patterns or as a comparable effect size.
- Preferred language: “changed,” “coincided,” “descriptive gradient,” “consistent with,” and “cannot distinguish.” Avoid “caused,” “treatment,” and “effect” for this package's estimates.
- January 2023 is the end of German purchases of Russian pipeline oil, not the beginning of the broader energy shock.
- The decisive upgrade is a demonstrated municipality fuel-price first stage. Only then consider a regression and the spatial-analysis stationarity/SCPC protocol.
